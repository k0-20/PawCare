#  PawCare – AI-Assisted Pet Healthcare & Preventive Care Management

##  Project Overview

**PawCare** is an AI-assisted pet healthcare and animal welfare management platform designed to help pet owners organise and monitor their pets' health. It provides a centralised system for maintaining pet profiles, medical histories, vaccination records, treatments, and veterinary follow-ups.

The platform features a **Care-Gap Intelligence system** that evaluates available medical records and classifies preventive-care activities as *Completed, Due Soon, Overdue, Unknown, or Unverified*. By identifying upcoming vaccinations, overdue care, and missing or unverified information, PawCare helps pet owners manage preventive healthcare more reliably and prepare relevant information for veterinary consultations.

PawCare also aims to provide cautious, educational symptom guidance and timely reminders, making pet healthcare more organised, accessible, and easier to manage.

## Key Objectives

- **Centralised Pet Records:** Maintain pet profiles, medical histories, vaccinations, treatments, and follow-ups in one place.
- **Preventive Care Tracking:** Identify upcoming and overdue vaccinations, appointments, and recommended care activities.
- **Care-Gap Intelligence:** Classify healthcare status using rule-based logic and available record information.
- **Reliable Information Management:** Use validation, duplicate flags, and verification statuses to distinguish confirmed records from missing or unverified information.
- **AI-Assisted Guidance:** Provide explainable, educational pet-health information with appropriate safety precautions.
- **Veterinary Consultation Support:** Help owners organise relevant health records and documents before visiting a veterinarian.

##  How It Works

1. The pet owner logs in and selects an existing pet or registers a new one.
2. PawCare retrieves available medical records and validates the entered information.
3. The system checks record availability and verification status.
4. A rule-based algorithm evaluates the applicable preventive-care schedule.
5. Each care activity is classified as Completed, Due Soon, Overdue, Unknown, or Unverified.
6. The platform displays relevant alerts and allows the owner to update records.

##  Project Impact

PawCare aims to improve pet healthcare management by making medical information easier to organise, helping owners identify preventive-care gaps, and supporting better-prepared veterinary consultations. Its focus on record validation and verification helps distinguish completed care from missing or uncertain information.

**Important:** PawCare is intended to support pet owners with healthcare organisation and educational guidance; it does not replace professional veterinary diagnosis or treatment.
